DaFeiYu

A personal desktop tool for staging your own videos as TikTok drafts.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What it does

DaFeiYu runs locally on the operator's own Windows computer. You pick a video file from your machine, and DaFeiYu sends it to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You then open TikTok, review the video, write your caption, choose your audience, and publish it yourself.

DaFeiYu does not post on your behalf. It has no publish capability: it cannot make anything public, and it holds no permission that would let it try.

How it connects

Authorization uses TikTok Login Kit with the standard authorization-code flow and PKCE. The redirect is caught on a loopback address (http://127.0.0.1:3455/callback/) on the same machine — no public server is involved. The only TikTok permission requested is video.upload, which stages an inbox draft.

Where your data lives

Access and refresh tokens are written to local disk, encrypted with Windows DPAPI under the operator's own Windows account. There is no DaFeiYu server, no account system, and no analytics. The only network destination is open.tiktokapis.com.

Who can use it

This is a single-user tool. It is not distributed as a service, has no sign-up, and is operated only by its owner against their own TikTok account.
